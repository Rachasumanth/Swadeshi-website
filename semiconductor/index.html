RESEARCH // 06 • HARDWARE & SILICON

From Intelligence in Software to Intelligence in Silicon.

Exploring the physical frontier of machine intelligence. We study analog circuits, continuous signal flow, and transistor physics to design non-von-Neumann architectures that compute with physics rather than clock cycles.

SOFTWARE PARADIGM

Discrete Symbolic Logic

Boolean gating, digital ALU cycles, synchronous clocks, and discrete memory bus transfers between CPU/GPU and DRAM.

→ CONVERGENCE → ANALOG ACCELERATION
HARDWARE PARADIGM

Continuous Physical Computing

Kirchhoff current accumulation, sub-threshold conductance, continuous time state equations, and zero clock-skew memory.

STUDY 01

Sub-Threshold MOSFET Conduction

Analyzing exponential current-voltage curves when operating transistors below their threshold voltage, enabling ultra-low-power neuromorphic weighting.

STUDY 02

Analog Compute-in-Memory (CiM)

Directly performing matrix-vector multiplications within memory crossbar arrays, eliminating the massive energy cost of shuffling weights across buses.

STUDY 03

EDA & Open-Source Silicon Toolchains

Developing automated SPICE netlist synthesis bridges that translate high-level agent policy definitions into verifiable hardware layout descriptions.

Research & Development Status

Swadeshi AI Private Limited explicitly presents its semiconductor program as an exploratory R&D initiative. We do not claim commercial silicon production or active foundry tape-outs. Our current work focuses on mathematical modeling, transistor-level SPICE simulations, and theoretical circuit topologies.
