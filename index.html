A research-oriented technology laboratory established on 9 March 2026. We focus on difficult technical problems across intelligent software and deep-tech hardware.

Swadeshi AI is not an IT services vendor or a generic SaaS wrapper. We operate with the intellectual rigor of a scientific laboratory and the velocity of a deep-tech engineering group.
