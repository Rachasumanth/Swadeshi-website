Who We Are

About SwadeshiAI Private Limited

SwadeshiAI Private Limited is an Indian artificial intelligence startup on a mission to build globally competitive AI technologies — designed, developed, and owned in India.

We are building the foundational infrastructure for the next era of AI: multi-agent systems, autonomous workflows, and developer tools that empower creators to build intelligent applications without constraint.

Our work is rooted in the belief that India is not just a market for AI — it is a source of AI innovation. We are here to prove that world-class artificial intelligence can be built from India, for the world.
