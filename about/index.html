ABOUT // INSTITUTIONAL IDENTITY

Swadeshi AI Private Limited

A research-oriented technology laboratory established on 9 March 2026. We investigate difficult, high-leverage technical problems at the confluence of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity systems, and analog semiconductor electronics.

Institutional Positioning

Swadeshi AI is not an agency, outsourcing shop, or IT service provider. We are organized as a research laboratory dedicated to deep-tech engineering and computational self-reliance.

We believe the future of computation requires continuous integration across the technological stack: from high-level reasoning runtimes and standardized protocols (MCP) to defensive system verification, down to the continuous physics of analog silicon.

Commitment to Data Honesty

In a field saturated with inflated claims and corporate buzzwords, Swadeshi AI adheres to strict scientific truthfulness.

We do not invent historical awards, claim commercial silicon that has not yet been fabricated, or advertise fabricated partnerships. Our progress is measured by reproducible code, verified benchmark suites, and rigorous technical documentation.
